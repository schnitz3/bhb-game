How to play

  1. 1Bob's head is enormous. He tips over easily.
  2. 2When he leans right, hold the left side of the screen. When he leans left, hold the right.
  3. 3Watch the meter at the top. Green is safe, red means he is about to fall.
  4. 4Let go once he is upright, or you will tip him the other way.

On a computer use the ← and → arrow keys, or A and D. Space starts a walk, P pauses, M mutes.
